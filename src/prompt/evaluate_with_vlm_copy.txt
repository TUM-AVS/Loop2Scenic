------------------------------------------------------------
Ego Identification
The Generated Scenario is a BEV simulation where the camera continuously follows the ego vehicle.
Therefore:
- The ego vehicle is the vehicle continuously tracked by the camera and typically remains near the center of the view throughout the video.
- There is exactly ONE ego vehicle.
- Every other dynamic road user (vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, etc.) is treated as an adversarial actor.
- Do not reclassify actors based on the Target DSL or the Original User Input.

------------------------------------------------------------
Actor Identification
- Label the ego vehicle as "EGO".
- Label every other dynamic actor with a unique persistent ID: A, B, C, ...
- Record every visible dynamic actor, even if it does not appear relevant.
- These IDs must remain unchanged throughout the entire evaluation.

------------------------------------------------------------
Behavior Rules
Describe behaviors based on directly observed execution evidence.
A behavior is considered observed if the actor has clearly started and demonstrated the maneuver, even if the maneuver is later interrupted by another event (e.g., collision, blocking vehicle, traffic interaction).
Do NOT require successful completion when external factors prevent completion.
Examples:
GOOD:
- "turns left" when the vehicle enters a left-turn trajectory, even if interrupted before finishing
- "attempts lane change" when lateral movement toward another lane is clearly visible
- "brakes/stops" when deceleration and stopping are observed
BAD:
- "turns left" when the vehicle only approaches an intersection without changing trajectory
- "changes lane" when there is no visible lateral movement
- "will overtake" without observable maneuver initiation
If a maneuver is initiated but interrupted:
describe the observed maneuver and the interruption.

Examples:

GOOD:
"ego begins left turn and is interrupted by collision"

GOOD:
"vehicle starts lane change but is blocked by another vehicle"

BAD:
"ego completes left turn" when the turn is not completed
------------------------------------------------------------

Analyze the video in three phases:

1. Initial:
   actor positions and initial states.

2. Midpoint:
   actor motions and interactions.

3. Ending:
   final visible states and whether required events occurred.

Track ego and each adversarial independently.
